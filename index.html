Why I am running

Put people back at the center of public service.

America does not need more performance. It needs honest information, steady leadership, responsible stewardship, and solutions that reach families, workers, seniors, veterans, students, small businesses, and communities too often left unheard.

Jennifer Pearl 2028 is a campaign for transparent government, economic security, environmental renewal, accessible education, ethical technology, healthier communities, and a nation capable of solving difficult problems together.

“Leadership is the patient work of listening, building, repairing, and making sure every person can understand the government that serves them.”
Jennifer Kay Pearl
